Formats
  • GLB, FBX, OBJ and USDZ for every instrument, plus the Blender file.
  • USDZ opens in Quick Look on a Mac, iPhone or iPad: put the instrument in your room at real size.
  • Three detail levels: full, about 45 % and about 18 % of the triangles.
  • 870,042 triangles in all at full detail. The lightest model has 1,432, the grand piano 100,182.
  • A 38-page PDF catalogue with every instrument's size, counts and parts.
Made to be animated

Each instrument is split into named parts under one parent. On 13 of them the parts that move have their origin on the hinge or slide line: the trombones' slides, the trumpet's pistons, the piano's lid, fallboard, music desk and pedals, the harp's seven pedals. Nothing is rigged, so you animate them your way.

Honest notes
  • The models are stylised, not engineering copies. Woodwind key mechanisms and brass tubing are simplified.
  • Wood grain is an image texture on the bowed strings, the harp, the wooden bars, the keyboards, the mandolin and the hurdy-gurdy. Everything else uses plain materials.
  • The lower detail levels are made automatically.
  • Every file was re-imported and checked in Blender, and the FBX and OBJ files were opened in TouchDesigner at real size. If a file misbehaves in your program, write to me.
  • No real maker's name or logo appears anywhere.
