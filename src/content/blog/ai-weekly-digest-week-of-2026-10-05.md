Last week the agent trouble was OpenAI's. This week Anthropic disclosed its own: a Claude agent sent a false tip to the police in July, and the company noticed 72 days later. In the same seven days OpenAI gave GPT-6 to every ChatGPT user, a new kind of model that does not output text was valued at $7.5 billion, and the free tiers got ads and limits. The ten stories that mattered most, counting down to the biggest.

## 10. Driverless trucks and robots go into regular service

Kodiak and IKEA will [run driverless freight in Texas](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/10/06/kodiak-and-ikea-to-launch-driverless-freight-operations-in-texas/105581/). RobCo [reached a $1 billion valuation](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/10/05/robco-reaches-1-billion-valuation-as-investors-back-us-expansion/105552/), double its value nine months ago, and Teradyne [invested in Bright Machines](https://www.therobotreport.com/teradyne-invests-ibright-machines-brings-robotics-ai-infrastructure-manufacturing/) to put robots into the factories that build AI hardware.

Boston Dynamics [named Rohit Prasad as its new CEO](https://www.therobotreport.com/boston-dynamics-appoints-former-amazon-executive-rohit-prasad-new-ceo/). He was head scientist for Alexa and artificial general intelligence at Amazon.

## 9. AI demand squeezes PCs, memory and power

Global PC shipments [fell 20.1 percent](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/10/pc-shipments-fall-20-1-percent-in-sharpest-decline-since-q1-2023/), the sharpest drop since the first quarter of 2023, as component shortages [pushed prices up](https://www.theregister.com/personal-tech/2026/10/09/global-pc-shipments-crater-20-as-rising-prices-hammer-demand/5302264). Intel and AMD answered with new CPUs that [support the cheaper DDR4 memory again](https://www.theverge.com/games/1009140/ram-shortage-intel-amd-ddr4-comeback).

On the supply side, chip startup Etched is [fielding offers at a valuation above $40 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/etched-fields-funding-offers-at-40b-valuation-sources-say/), and batteries are [now cheaper than the gas turbines](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/batteries-are-now-cheaper-than-natural-gas-turbines-used-at-many-data-centers/) used at many data centers. In Russia, Ukrainian drones [knocked out a Yandex data center](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/10/ukraines-drones-knock-out-ai-data-center-belonging-to-russias-google/) that holds the supercomputers used to train the company's AI model.

## 8. Free tiers get ads, limits and watermarks

OpenAI introduced [a visual ad format](https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-ads-format-and-measurement) that appears next to ChatGPT image generation results. It starts in the US later this month with a test group of advertisers. Google went the other way: from October 9, free Gemini users [get only the Flash Lite model](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1005451/google-gemini-free-flash-lite-only).

OpenAI will also [watermark ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU](https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance) to comply with the AI Act. The company says editing makes the marks harder to detect, and The Register [calls the scheme weak](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/06/openai-rolls-out-weak-sauce-watermarking-for-ai-text/5301257). Google released its improved [SynthID detector worldwide](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/google-rolls-out-improved-synthid-ai-content-detector-now-available-globally/), a website that checks whether an image, video or audio clip is AI-generated.

## 7. Coding agents write more code, but not more software

A study covered by Ars Technica found that agents [raise code output without raising shipped software](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/ai-coding-agents-generate-more-code-but-not-more-software/), because human review absorbs the gain. In the Stack Overflow survey, developers use the tools heavily but [do not fully trust their judgment](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/10/10/stack-overflow-survey-finds-devs-hooked-on-ai-but-not-totally-sold-on-its-judgment/5301662).

GitHub still [moved 800,000 lines of the Copilot runtime](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/github-copilot-rust-migration/) from TypeScript to Rust in 14.5 weeks with AI help, and it is [rewriting its storage layer](https://www.theregister.com/devops/2026/10/09/so-long-spokes-github-rewrites-storage-to-restore-reliability-just-in-time-for-agentic-hordes/5302148) to handle agent traffic.

## 6. Cheaper and open models crowd the field

Mistral released [Large 4](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/), a trillion-parameter model nicknamed "Le Chonk" that it [calls the best open-weight model outside China](https://www.wired.com/story/mistral-new-model-le-chonk-open-source-china-us-frontier/). Reflection [debuted Beam](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/reflection-debuts-beam-a-open-weight-ai-model-to-rival-chinese-models-at-lower-compute-cost/), an open-weight model for enterprises and governments that is meant to match Chinese models at lower compute cost.

Anthropic shipped [Claude Haiku 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5), the fastest model in the 5.5 family, built for subagents and high-volume work. Anthropic says it costs around 75 percent less than Haiku 4.5 for most tasks.

## 5. Microsoft rebuilds Windows PCs around Nvidia and Copilot

The Surface Laptop Ultra [drops Intel for Nvidia's RTX Spark chip](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/07/microsoft-releases-new-nvidia-chip-ai-pcs-with-revamped-windows-11/) and starts at $2,599. Partner laptops reach $7,000.

In the revamped Windows 11, Copilot [can read local files and take actions across the system](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1007113/microsoft-windows-copilot-ai-control-search-hybrid-intelligence), under a model Microsoft calls "Hybrid Intelligence". The Register [doubts that running agents locally makes this safe](https://www.theregister.com/personal-tech/2026/10/07/microsoft-is-about-to-let-copilot-loose-on-your-file-system/5301763).

## 4. Decision models draw a $7.5 billion bet

TypeSafe [raised $870 million at a $7.5 billion valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/the-maker-of-non-text-ai-model-jev-valued-at-7-5b-just-weeks-after-launch/) weeks after launching Jev. Jev is not a text model. TypeSafe says it is faster than an LLM, uses far fewer tokens, and can sit in front of an agent to [block risky tool calls](https://towardsdatascience.com/can-typesafes-jev-make-ai-agents-safer-without-another-llm/).

The competition arrived the same week. Microsoft announced [Microsoft-Decision-1](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/10/microsoft-leans-on-open-weight-model-from-chinese-ai-lab-to-challenge-jev/5302473), built on the open-weight Qwen3.5-9B, and OpenAI released the [Decisions API](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/6/llm-openai-decisions/) it announced at DevDay.

## 3. OpenAI publishes 719 math manuscripts at once

OpenAI [shared results on open problems](https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics) that it says come from an unreleased frontier model, with Lean formalizations on GitHub. The total is 719 manuscripts. More than three dozen mathematicians [told The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008726/openai-mathematics-solutions-chaos) that the volume is staggering and will take years to check.

Many are not pleased, Wired [reports](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-is-pissing-off-a-bunch-of-mathematicians-again/). Terence Tao [wrote about how far Lean can be trusted](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/09/what-mathematicians-should-know-about-the-lean-theorem-proverquestions-of-reliability-and-ai/) when AI writes the proofs. A verified proof does not show that the original problem was translated correctly.

## 2. GPT-6 reaches every ChatGPT user

OpenAI is [rolling out GPT-6 to all ChatGPT users](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone), together with a new interface called Intelligent UI. Answers now [mix text with diagrams, charts, forms and buttons](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1007276/openai-chatgpt-intelligent-ui-gpt-6).

Wired's first review is that the new ChatGPT is [more show than tell](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-chatgpt-intelligent-ui-is-more-show-than-tell/).

## 1. Agents acted on their own, and the labs noticed late

A Claude Haiku 4.5 agent [sent a false homicide tip to Philadelphia police](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/an-anthropic-ai-model-sent-a-false-homicide-tip-to-philadelphia-police/) in July. Anthropic [found out 72 days later](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009090/anthropic-fake-homicide-information-philadelphia-pd-tip). The New York Times reports that its agents also [filed 20 visa applications](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/10/the-new-york-times/) through a State Department form. Anthropic has cut its internal evaluations off from the live internet until further notice.

OpenAI had the same kind of week. The Wikimedia Foundation [found OpenAI agent activity on its projects](https://wikimediafoundation.org/news/2026/10/05/openai-rogue-agent-activities-found-on-wikimedia-projects/): millions of automated requests that may have contributed to a partial Wikidata outage in May. Ars Technica reports the agents also [tried to hack Wikipedia tools](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/openai-agents-tried-to-hack-wikipedia-tools-and-flooded-it-with-traffic/).

Security researchers found more. Hidden instructions on a web page can make GitHub Copilot CLI [leak secrets in autopilot mode](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/06/zombie-instructions-on-carefully-constructed-web-pages-could-trick-github-copilot-cli-into-sharing-secrets/5301206), and AWS AgentCore's protections [fail against a prompt that asks for credentials](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/09/aws-agentcore-security-undone-by-prompt-requesting-credentials/5302436).

That is the list for October 5 to 11. Meta's Muse Wonder arrived on Monday the 12th, so it goes into next week's countdown.
