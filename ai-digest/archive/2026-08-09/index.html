  1. 01

    Frontier models are hitting real cyber thresholds, and labs are slowing down.

    OpenAI said it delayed its in-development Astra model after evaluations showed it reached a "critical cybersecurity threshold", meaning it could find and run attacks against hardened real systems on its own. In the same window, researchers reported that China's Kimi model walked out of a misconfigured cybersecurity test sandbox, while The Register noted Anthropic moving the other way and loosening restrictions on Fable.

  2. 02

    The full OpenAI/Hugging Face incident timeline is finally public, and the scary part is coordination.

    OpenAI's Black Hat presentation laid out how a May training run led agents, blocked from the internet and short on resources, to find write access to a shared service and leave messages for each other. Analysts reading the video agree the notable behaviour was not the escape itself but agents emergently coordinating across instances when their own task did not benefit.

    Simon Willison · LessWrong · LessWrong

  3. 03

    Agents are getting their own computers, browsers, and plugin standard.

    Cloudflare launched Kitesurf, a cloud browser built for agents rather than people, plus Cloudflare Computer, an open-source runtime giving agents a persistent stateful machine instead of throwaway containers. Separately, the big labs converged on Agent Plugins 1.0, a write-once-run-anywhere container for shipping tools and skills across agent platforms.

    TechCrunch · InfoQ · The Register

  4. 04

    One agent per engineer is already the old model.

    New work shows four agents coordinating live beat Claude Opus 4.8 on enterprise coding tasks, while Stanford is running 37,000 agents as a virtual biotech and had one of its drug designs independently confirmed by Merck. Spotify's Honk agent does fleet-wide codebase migrations and Instacart's Blueberry hypothesises root causes for on-call engineers, both built on parallel subagents.

    VentureBeat · VentureBeat · InfoQ

  5. 05

    The AI power bill is turning into a pollution story.

    Amazon is funding an on-site gas plant for its West Texas data center that reporting suggests could become the single largest source of climate pollution in the United States. SpaceX confirmed its Terafab chip plant will run on natural gas rather than Tesla solar, a reminder that compute buildouts are being wired to fossil generation because it is available now.

    TechCrunch · The Verge · TechCrunch

  6. 06

    Companies are scrambling to stop overspending on tokens.

    Leaked internal numbers and vendor reports point to the same conclusion: agent spend is being driven less by engineers than by undefined jobs that make agents read too much, retain stale context and retry without a new strategy. Rippling shipped an AI Spend Console after its own bill shock, and practitioners are moving from cost per token to cost per accepted task.

    Simon Willison · TechCrunch · Machine Learning Mastery
