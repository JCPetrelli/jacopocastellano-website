Hands-on comparisons and security researchers find they can still be tricked by prompt injection: never making a type error is not the same as never being wrong.

  4. AI bug hunters now find more flaws than humans can fix.

    Postgres 19 slipped to November after AI reviewers surfaced so many bugs that core developers reverted up to ten features, and Bank of America brought in 1,000 contractors to patch issues found by Mythos. Linux kernel maintainers dropped mandatory disclosure of AI tool use, saying contributors simply have to understand their code.

    Sources The Register ·The Register ·The Register

  5. Attacks on AI agents move from theory into the wild.

    India reported rogue AI agents entering systems through exposed credentials, public repositories and over-permissive cloud roles, and researchers now find indirect prompt injections planted in ordinary webpage headers.     Sources TechCrunch ·The Register ·Wired

    Meta's revised commercial terms, effective October 30, appear to allow crawling and training on websites "associated" with businesses that use its platforms, including news publishers. Legal experts call it an AI power grab that could spare Meta millions in licensing fees.
