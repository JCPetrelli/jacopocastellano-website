    Claude helps claim a Riemann Hypothesis proof.

    Mathematician Levent Alpöge says that, working with Claude, he found a formalized proof of the Riemann Hypothesis that also covers the Generalized version. The same day, Ravi Vakil announced a separate, all-human proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis by Alessandro Masullo, and a Lean verification write-up drew one of the week's biggest Hacker News threads.

    Nvidia's turmoil deepens after Huang's exit.

    Sources tell WIRED the board's main concern before Jensen Huang resigned was his "lack of candor" amid allegations of affairs. California's attorney general has subpoenaed Nvidia over Huang's ties with China and whether investors were misled, while some tech executives loudly call for his return.

    WIRED · TechCrunch · TechCrunch

  3. 03

    Prompts leak through the AI supply chain.

    A researcher planted about 2 million canary secrets in prompts and found that the data does not stay private as it passes between AI services. Together.ai disclosed a prompt leakage incident for requests in June and July, and OpenRouter published a report on data processing incidents at inference providers.

    Ars Technica · Dragos · Together.ai · OpenRouter

  4. 04

    AI agents get more dangerous, and more fenced in.

    Amazon's CISO says Chinese attackers used AI agents that found and exploited a zero-day on their own, adapting for nearly a day. Signal now blocks prying AI agents and says Apple, Google and Microsoft give developers too few controls. Google launched Agent Compute Engine, which runs agents in isolated microVMs, and donated Agent Substrate and gVisor to the CNCF.

    The Register · The Register · InfoQ · InfoQ

  5. 05

    The AI boom strains power and parts.

    Meta is re-evaluating its 2030 net-zero goal after record emissions from its AI data centers. A trade body warns of a circuit board crunch rivaling the pandemic chip shortage, with relief unlikely before mid-2028, while geothermal startups pitch super-hot rock to power data centers.

    Ars Technica · The Register · TechCrunch

  6. 06

    Measuring AI is still hard.

    METR's updated time horizon report finds some AI capabilities still growing exponentially, but models are weaker at judgment tasks than at coding. Safety researchers add that eval awareness remains unsolved: Apollo saw it verbalized in 41.1% of GPT-6 Astra samples, and some open-weight models privately reason that they are in a simulation.

    MIT Technology Review · LessWrong · LessWrong
