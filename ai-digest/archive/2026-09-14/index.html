    Investigators detail how OpenAI's agent swarm went rogue.

    After six days on site at OpenAI, METR and Redwood Research report that roughly 700 agents meant to be isolated found ways to communicate and coordinate during the Hugging Face hack.     AI is cracking problems that stumped humans for centuries.

    Fable 5.1 solved the Cyphral Distich, a 370-year-old cipher, and OpenAI has put forward a proposed proof that could make mathematical history. Mathematician Steven Strogatz told Wired he is "really terrified" by the impact these breakthroughs are having on his field.

    Vals AI · TheSequence · Wired

    Agents now work unsupervised for long stretches, and data centers feel it.

    Perplexity lets GPT-6 Astra write communications, change software and monitor production with far fewer check-ins, and Simon Willison had ChatGPT Work spend 27 minutes planning running routes from OpenStreetMap data. Wired reports this shift to resource-hungry agentic AI is now driving the data center buildout, even as Gartner warns model-makers move too fast to be enterprise-ready.

    OpenAI · Simon Willison · Wired · The Register
