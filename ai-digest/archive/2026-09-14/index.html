    Sources Vals AI ·TheSequence ·Wired

Wired reports this shift to resource-hungry agentic AI is now driving the data center buildout, even as Gartner warns model-makers move too fast to be enterprise-ready.

    Sources OpenAI ·Simon Willison ·Wired ·The Register
