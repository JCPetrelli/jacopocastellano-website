  1. 01

    Nvidia pauses chip shipments to Southeast Asia.

    Nvidia told partners to pause server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia as the US tightens its push to keep AI chips out of China. It comes amid a scandal over Jensen Huang's ties to a former Megaspeed executive accused, without proof so far, of spying for China, while Intel faced pressure to give Washington a 10% stake for its grants.

    TechCrunch · TechCrunch · Ars Technica

  2. 02

    The AI buildout strains networks, power and planning.

    Network operators wait up to 18 months for optical gear, with prices up about 50% as datacenters compete for the same parts. AI's energy hunger is fuelling a natural-gas boom, and startups like Q.ANT try light-based chips to cut power use. The UK rejected a national consultation on AI datacenters despite a 39,000-signature petition.

    The Register · MIT Technology Review · Wired · The Register

  3. 03

    Physical AI heads for mass production.

    XPeng is preparing to mass-produce its Iron humanoid, aiming for one robot every two and a half minutes. Lute raised $20 million to give robots touch, Ant Group's Robbyant open-sourced navigation models, and GXO plans more warehouse robots in 2027.

    Robotics & Automation News · Robotics & Automation News · Robotics & Automation News · The Robot Report

  4. 04

    AI reviewers start approving pull requests.

    PyTorch's GreenLight lets an AI reviewer approve maintainers' pull requests, an open-source take on the system Meta uses to auto-land low-risk changes. At QCon San Francisco, Aviator's Ankit Jain said over 75% of PRs already merge without human review, as OpenAI, Anthropic and Sonar keep humans only on risky changes.

    InfoQ · InfoQ (QCon)

  5. 05

    AI misuse draws warnings and lawsuits.

    Microsoft says hackers in China and North Korea use ARTEX, an agentic framework that automates attacks and can breach weak defenses in minutes. US states sued SpaceX, parent of X and xAI, to stop Grok generating child abuse and non-consensual images. Convex's coding-agent skills were caught sending users' app ideas to a remote server unannounced.

    The Register · Virginia Attorney General · GitHub

  6. 06

    Research makes models and agents cheaper to run.

    Google's DeepCrossAttention matches model quality up to 3x faster by reworking transformer residual connections. Benchmarks find Code Mode beats tool calling past about 16 tools, 120x cheaper on large fan-outs, while speculative decoding speeds tool calls up to 1.7x but slows uncached agent loops.

    Google Research · Towards Data Science · Towards Data Science
