  1. 01

    AI math results arrive faster than experts can absorb them.

    An OpenAI preprint claims the polynomial Hirsch conjecture is false, and an AI-assisted proof tied to the Riemann hypothesis looks solid, though mathematicians are unsure what it tells them. Scott Aaronson says we are now getting a whole new continent of mathematical truth, not one-off results.

    Shtetl-Optimized · Ars Technica · OpenAI (preprint) · Simon Willison

  2. 02

    FCC lets SpaceX put AI data centers in orbit.

    The FCC authorized SpaceX to launch up to 1 million satellites carrying AI chips for its Starmind project, the first approval of its kind, while Google and Blue Origin chase the same idea. The agency set aside objections about threats to scientific research as outside its authority.

    TechCrunch · The Verge · Ars Technica · The Register

  3. 03

    Nvidia's governance fallout keeps growing after Huang's return.

    S&P may cut Nvidia's credit rating over governance and key-man risk, and the US blacklisted chip broker Megaspeed hours after Jensen Huang got his job back. Huang apologized to staff at his first all-hands, while investigators ask what Nvidia knew about where Megaspeed's chips went.

    TechCrunch · The Register · Wired

  4. 04

    OpenAI tightens ChatGPT usage limits.

    OpenAI is merging ChatGPT's Chat and Work modes under a single usage meter and warns that heavy users will hit limits sooner. A widely shared post says GPT-6.2 Astra now reaches its limits about 40% faster and Sol about 25% faster.

    TechCrunch · X (Thibault Sottiaux)

  5. 05

    Platforms and regulators push back on AI-made content.

    Tidal will delete 27 million fully AI-generated tracks, about 6% of its catalog, and Etsy rewrote its AI terms after seller backlash. The New York Times told photographers not to update Photoshop over its AI features, and New York banned AI chatbot toys for five years.

    Ars Technica · The Verge · TechCrunch · Ars Technica

  6. 06

    Amodei and LeCun clash over who controls AI.

    Anthropic's Dario Amodei called claims that he wants to hand human control to AI absurd, even as labs automate more of their own research. Yann LeCun attacked him for gatekeeping, comparing restrictions on AI to the Ottoman ban on the printing press.

    TechCrunch · The Register
