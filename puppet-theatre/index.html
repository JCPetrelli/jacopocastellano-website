Light for your stage.

Video projections built into the set of your show. Rain on a window, leaves in the wind, a storm coming in: played live, in the rhythm of your performance.

Raindrop: a drop, a window, rain, leaves and a storm. Each scene is cued live.

The idea

Projection as part of the stage.

Light that falls on your scenery and blends with fabric, paper and shadow. It makes the world of your story bigger and changes the weather, the season or the time of day in a second.

Part of the set

The image lands on the scenery itself: a backdrop, a gauze, a shadow screen. It becomes part of the stage you already built.

Your story comes first

Projection gives space and mood: sky, weather, time of day, a whole world around the scene. It supports your story instead of telling it for you.

Played live

I run the projections myself during the show, following your performance. Nothing runs on by itself when the audience laughs or calls out.

How I work

  1. 01

    Your story

    I watch your piece and find the moments where light can tell something: rain, night, a forest that grows.

  2. 02

    Images for your stage

    I design the scenes for your set and its measurements, in your colours.

  3. 03

    Light setup

    Little equipment, set up in about ten minutes. The darkened room you already play in is enough.

  4. 04

    On stage

    I set everything up with you and play the projections live at every performance, cue by cue with your show.
